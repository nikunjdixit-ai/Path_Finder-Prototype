    How it works

    From goal to guided plan in three steps.

    1. Understand

      We take in the goal you pick, your experience level and the skills you already have.

    2. Map

      Your known skills are checked against what the goal actually requires, so the gaps are precise, not generic.

    3. Adapt

      Each gap is paired with real resources, and your path updates as you mark modules complete.

    Learning approach

    Built around real skill gaps, not generic courses.

    Skill-gap detection

    We compare what your goal needs against what you already know, so you only spend time on the gaps.

    Progress that adapts

    Mark modules complete as you go — your dashboard and remaining gaps update to match.
